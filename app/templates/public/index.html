Why a script matters

The idea is there. The script is the work.

A story often starts as a scene in your head, a page of notes or a draft that nobody has read yet. A comic script asks for more. You have to decide what each panel shows, which words belong to which character and where one scene gives way to the next.

TOLSTOY takes that first pass and puts it in a form you can edit. The story choices stay with you.

How it works

From brief to script in three steps

Step 1

Write the brief

Add a title and your story idea or story text. Choose the character gender, audience, length and language you want.

Step 2

Generate the script

Gemini turns the brief into structured JSON, split into scenes and panels with dialogue and narration.

Step 3

Read it and keep working

A new story stays in EDITING while you revise it. Mark it COMPLETED when it is ready, or move it back if you want another pass.

Workspace

What you get in the workspace

A script with structure

The generated script is JSON text. Scenes, panels, dialogue and narration each have their place, so you can read it or work from the raw output.

A story you can revisit

EDITING and COMPLETED are separate from the generation result. Finishing a story does not lock it.

A balance you can follow

Your token balance sits in the user area. New accounts get 5 tokens, and 5 more arrive every 48 hours without resetting what is left.

Rules enforced on the server

Login uses Flask sessions. Changes are protected by CSRF tokens, and access checks run in the backend for every request.

Tokens

How tokens work

Generating a script spends tokens. The server works out the cost from a central setting before the request runs, so the browser never decides the price. If a generation fails after the deduction, the tokens go back to your balance.

FAQ

Questions before you start

Does TOLSTOY draw the comic?

No. It writes the script. The output is text with scenes, panels, dialogue and narration, no images.

What do I get when I sign up?

An account with 5 tokens and a workspace for your stories. You can write a brief, generate a script and edit it there.

What happens after 48 hours?

5 tokens are added to your balance. They stack on top of whatever you have left, rather than replacing it.

Can I change a script after generating it?

Yes. Stories stay in EDITING while you work on them. Move one to COMPLETED when you are done and back to EDITING whenever you want to revise it.

Who decides what a generation costs?

The server, using one shared setting for all accounts. The page cannot change that number, and a failed generation is refunded.
